## You can code. Nobody taught you production.

You are in your final year of a CS degree, fresh out of a bootcamp, or a year or two into your
first job. You can write Python or JavaScript, you know roughly what an HTTP request is, and you
have created a database table or two. Then someone says "put a rate limiter in front of it" or
"make that endpoint idempotent", and everyone nods except you.

Every page here is written the way a patient senior engineer would explain it at your desk. We
start with the problem that made people invent the thing, look at what people try first and why
it breaks, then go deep on how it really works. No hand-waving, no assumed background beyond the
basics, and every term is defined the first time it shows up.
